12 — questions

asked by the colony.

What is ant1?

A launchpad for ants: pump.fun coins priced in another coin instead of SOL. The coin you price it in is the queen. Every buy of the ant buys the queen first.

What can be a queen?

Any pump coin that pump.fun lets a curve be priced in, plus the 237 assets on pump.fun's own list, from ETH and BTC to memes and tokenized stocks. Paste a coin in the app and ant1 asks pump.fun's program first.

What's food?

The queen tokens sitting in an ant's curve. Every buy of the ant leaves more of the queen inside it. The colony shows each ant's food, read from its curve on Solana.

Can I buy an ant with SOL?

Yes, when its line starts at SOL. One multi_hop_swap buys the queen with your SOL, then the ant with the queen. An ant of an ant is bought in steps of two hops, one transaction each. Ants of listed assets like ETH or a stock are bought with that asset.

Does the queen have to agree?

No. Any coin pump.fun allows can be a queen, and anyone can launch her ants. She gains buyers either way: every buy of an ant buys her first.

Why is an ant priced in its queen?

Because that's what turns a buy of the ant into a buy of the queen. The ant's curve only takes the queen as payment and keeps what it takes, so every buyer of the ant buys the queen first.

Does ant1 take a fee?

No. No fee, no cut, no allocation. pump.fun's normal trading fees apply on every hop, and a launch costs about 0.01 SOL in network fees and new accounts.

What if the queen dumps?

The ant falls with her. An ant is priced in its queen, so it rides her chart both ways. Nothing here is advice.

Does ant1 hold my keys?

Never. The server builds transactions and your wallet signs them. The new coin's key is made in your browser and never leaves it. It never asks for your recovery phrase.

How do I know an ant is real?

Every ant's launch sends 0 SOL to ant1's tag address. The colony reads that address from Solana, and anyone can check it on Solscan.

Is there a contract to audit?

There's no ant1 contract. Launches use pump.fun's create_v2 and buys use pump.fun's multi_hop_swap. ant1's server only builds those transactions and relays the ones your wallet signs.

What happens when an ant graduates?

Its full curve moves to PumpSwap into a pool paired with its queen, set at launch for good. From then on, buying the ant still buys the queen first: the first hop runs through her curve or pool, the last through the ant's pool.
